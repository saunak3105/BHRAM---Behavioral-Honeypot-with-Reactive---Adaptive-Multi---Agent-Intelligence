About BHRAM Technologies

Founded in 2011, we are a global cybersecurity company headquartered in Bengaluru, India.

Our Mission

BHRAM Technologies exists to make enterprise-grade cybersecurity accessible, intelligent, and adaptive. We believe that the future of security lies not in reactive measures, but in proactive, behavior-driven defense systems that evolve with the threat landscape.

Our History

BHRAM Technologies was incorporated in 2011 by a team of former DRDO and IIT researchers with a shared vision: build security technology that thinks like an attacker. From our first managed SOC deployment in 2013 to our AI-driven threat platform launched in 2022, we have grown to serve over 500 enterprise clients across 42 countries.

Technology Philosophy

We operate on the principle of "assume breach" — designing all our systems to detect, deceive, and contain attackers even when perimeter defenses have been bypassed. Our proprietary MITRE ATT&CK alignment engine classifies attacker behavior in real time, enabling our SOC analysts to respond with surgical precision.
